The board takeaway

Danone and Unilever are the clearest premium-sustainability models.

Both portfolios skew toward differentiation, where sustainability compounds pricing power, mix and brand equity.

Nestlé is the scale platform.

It is the only player with enough breadth to win in both affordability and differentiation at the same time.

Coke, PepsiCo and Mondelez need cost-positive ESG.

Their mass portfolios mean sustainability has to pay back through efficiency, packaging, sourcing and share defense.
